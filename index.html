The Future

📌 Where I Started

Like many in IT, I started with the basics: resetting passwords, imaging computers, and learning that turning it off and on again actually works 90% of the time. But in that helpdesk chaos, I found my passion for solving problems and helping people.

🚀 The Breakthrough

The leap from support to engineering came from curiosity and continuous learning. I pursued certifications, built labs in my spare time, and volunteered for every infrastructure project I could find. Each challenge taught me something new.

💡 What Drives Me Now

Today, I focus on building scalable, secure, and efficient infrastructure. But more importantly, I'm driven by the opportunity to help others find their path—just like mentors did for me.

🎯 Looking Ahead

The next chapter is about innovation and leadership. I want to design systems that don't just work—they inspire. And I want to build a world where anyone, regardless of background, can have a thriving IT career.
